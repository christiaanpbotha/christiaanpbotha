# Nadine Hairdressing website

A one-page website for Nadine Hairdressing, a hair, nails and beauty salon at 85 Hennie Alberts St, Brackenhurst, Alberton.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs no build step and can go on any static host (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, cPanel).

## What's on the page

- **Hero:** her script wordmark, the tagline, the hours and the rose-wall photo
- **Services:** six categories taken from her Google Business listing
- **Bridal:** an enquiry form that opens WhatsApp with the details filled in
- **Gallery:** her Google photos, with filters for colour, cuts and bridal
- **Salon:** the interior and the product brands she uses
- **Booking:** a four-step booking request (services → day → time → details) that sends to WhatsApp. Days are limited to her trading hours (Tue–Fri 8–17, Sat 8–16).
- **Visit:** address, a directions link, hours with an "open now" indicator, both phone numbers
- **Mobile:** a sticky Call / WhatsApp / Book bar

## Brand

- **Colours** (from her sign and interior): ink `#1C1A1B`, white `#FAF8F6`, blush `#E8C6C3`, rose gold `#C8A28C`, champagne `#F1E5D3`, dove grey `#D9D6D7`
- **Fonts:** Great Vibes (her name only), Cormorant Garamond (headings), Jost (body text)
